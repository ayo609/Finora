Frequently Asked Questions
How do I create an account?
To create an account, click on the "Create Account" button on the registration page. Fill in your full name, username, mobile number, email address, and password. You can also add a referral code if you were referred by someone. After agreeing to the terms, click "Create Account" and you're done!
How do I deposit funds into my account?
To deposit funds, go to the "Top Up" page from your dashboard. Select the amount you want to deposit or enter a custom amount. Click "Proceed" to see the bank account details. Make a transfer to the provided account and upload your payment proof. Your deposit will be reviewed and approved by our admin team.
How do investments work?
We offer two types of investment plans: 5-day plans and 2-week plans. 5-day plans offer 40% ROI, while 2-week plans offer 100% ROI. Once you purchase a plan from your deposit wallet, your funds are locked for the duration. After the maturity period, your returns are automatically credited to your withdrawal wallet.
How do I withdraw my earnings?
To withdraw, go to the "Cashout" page. Enter the amount you want to withdraw (minimum ₦2,000). Your saved bank account will be used for the transfer. Enter your 4-digit withdrawal PIN to confirm. Withdrawals are processed between 8:30 AM and 5:30 PM. A 4% processing fee applies.
How does the referral program work?
Share your referral link with friends. When they register using your link and make their first investment, you earn: 10% from Level 1 referrals, 2% from Level 2, and 1% from Level 3. This is a one-time bonus credited to your withdrawal wallet on their first investment.
What is the Star Bonus program?
The Star Bonus rewards active referrers. When you have a certain number of active investors (people you referred who have purchased plans), you become eligible for daily bonuses ranging from ₦2,000 to ₦50,000. Check the Star Bonus page from your profile to see your progress and claim eligible bonuses.
How do I set up my withdrawal PIN?
Go to your Profile page and click on "Withdrawal PIN". If it's your first time, you can set a new 4-digit PIN using the numeric keypad. If you need to reset an existing PIN, you'll need to verify your login password first for security.
How long does deposit approval take?
Deposits are typically reviewed and approved within a few minutes to a few hours during working hours (8:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday). Make sure you upload a clear screenshot of your payment proof to speed up the process.
What should I do if I forget my password?
On the login page, enter your username or phone number and click "Forgot password?". We'll send a password reset link to your registered email address. Click the link to set a new password and regain access to your account.
Is there a minimum deposit amount?
Yes, the minimum deposit amount is ₦1,000. The minimum withdrawal amount is ₦2,000. Investment plans start from ₦5,000 for 5-day plans and ₦100,000 for 2-week plans.
